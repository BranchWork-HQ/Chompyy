The problem

The window resets whether you use it or not.

Your Claude plan gives you a 5-hour window that starts with your first message. Lunch, meetings, a night's sleep: whatever you didn't use when it resets is gone. Chompyy turns that leftover into finished work.

How it works

Park it. Approve it. Walk away.

Everything happens on a board in a small Mac app. Nothing runs until you approve it.

Park an idea

"Add tests for the date parser." Pick the repo, write it the way you'd say it.

Read the blueprint

Chompyy drafts the plan as a picture first: the goal, what changes, what it won't touch, how it checks itself.

Step away

Press I'm away, or set a bedtime. Approved cards run one at a time inside the window you have left.

Keep or undo

Come back to a review column. See the change, keep it into your branch or throw it away, and rate it.

In your menu bar

Always one glance away.

Built to run while nobody's watching

Careful by default.

Never past your limit

A budget gate checks the window before every card and never spends past 100%, so no surprise extra usage.

Works on a copy

Each card runs in its own git worktree and branch. Your working tree is never touched until you press Keep.

Sandboxed, nothing outward

No push, no publish, no network by default, and your credential folders are unreadable to the agent.

Checks the work itself

Chompyy runs your tests again in a sandbox after each card, instead of taking the agent's word for it.

One bad card never eats the night

Each card runs in its own process. A card that loops, hangs or crashes is stopped and the next one starts.

Learns your taste

What you keep, undo and rate becomes a taste.md that future cards follow, but only after you say yes.

Opening it the first time

Chompyy isn't notarized by Apple yet, so macOS asks once:

  1. Open Chompyy.dmg and drag Chompyy to Applications.
  2. Open Chompyy. macOS says it can't verify it: press Done.
  3. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, press Open Anyway.
  4. A 🍪 appears in your menu bar. Click it, then Open board.

Prefer the terminal? The CLI does everything the app does:

# needs Node.js 20+
npm install -g chompyy
chompyy status   # reads your quota
chompyy app      # installs the menu bar app
