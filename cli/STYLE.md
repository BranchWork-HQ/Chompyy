# How Chompyy writes

Every string a person reads: cards, notifications, hook messages, CLI output. Adapted from [i-have-adhd](https://github.com/ayghri/i-have-adhd) (MIT, © ayghri), for a reader who is in the middle of something else.

1. **Action first.** The first words are something to do: `/compact now`, `Approve`, `chompyy undo 34`. Reasons come after.
2. **One number that matters.** Use your own tokens, counted, e.g. "180k rewritten". No percentages of percentages.
3. **Two lines or fewer for a notification or hook message.** A card's front gets one short sentence per step. The evidence goes under "Details".
4. **Five items at most** in any list. Rank them and keep the rest behind `--all` or a click.
5. **One next step at the end.** If something is left open, name one thing that takes under two minutes.
6. **Say when.** Give a date or time ("verdict Dec 11", "cold at 14:20"), not "soon" or "two weeks on".
7. **Show wins and losses as they are.** "It worked: 309 fewer tokens each session start" or "13 still load". Never bury them.
8. **No preamble, recap or sign-off.** Drop "Chompyy noticed…", "Note that…" and "Hope this helps".
9. **No internal names.** Rule IDs (C1, T2), "rung" and "lane" stay in code and `--json`.
10. **Plain cause and fix for errors.** "settings.json isn't valid JSON: nothing changed."

Check a string by reading only its first line. If that line doesn't tell you what to do, rewrite it.
